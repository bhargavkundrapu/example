# Student Notebook System

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M3-L1
- **Lesson Title:** Student Notebook System
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 15 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can set up a **notebook system** (one inbox, one weekly review, one archive) that survives a full semester.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M3-L1-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

ChatGPT-type assistant + textbook PDF

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Treat knowledge like a tiny product: labels, dashboards, actions that survive exam week.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M3-L1-draft`.

---

## SEC-08_BAD_WORKFLOW

You memorise GPT paragraphs for oral exam without forming muscle memory.

---

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> Viva stalls at “Explain trade-off” — hollow eyes.

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Create areas: "Inbox", "Courses/ModuleName", "Archive", "Templates".
2. Default capture: **one** mobile shortcut to inbox.
3. Tag rule: #course #week #type(reading|lab|idea).
4. Weekly 20m: move inbox → course; delete noise.
5. Optional: AI suggests tag normalisation from a paste of your tag list — you approve.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
I describe my subjects + devices. Propose a folder/tag system with max 6 rules I can remember. Avoid enterprise complexity.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Here is a dump of my messy note titles (paste). Cluster into 5 buckets + suggest rename pattern — I choose final names.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Write a 10-line “weekly review script” I read aloud.
```

## SEC-15_CHALLENGE_TASK

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Over-nesting folders day one.
2. No weekly review — entropy wins.
3. Different systems per course — mental tax.
4. Tag spam (#everything).
5. Sensitive uploads to shared team space by mistake.

## SEC-18_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- **Inbox + review** beats perfect taxonomy.
- Few rules you obey > many you ignore.
