## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Treat knowledge like a tiny product: labels, dashboards, actions that survive exam week.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Pick 5 old notes max per session.
2. For each: one-line intent (“why I saved this”).
3. AI proposes summary + 3 tags — you edit.
4. Merge duplicates by hand—AI only flags similarity.
5. Archive originals if policy allows; else link.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Note text (paste). Give: 3-bullet summary • 3 tag suggestions • 1 “action or archive” decision — I will choose.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
These two notes (paste A, B). Are they duplicates? If partial overlap, say what to merge vs keep separate.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Turn summary into a **task** line: verb + object + due hint — not full project plan.
```

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Letting AI rename private people without consent.
2. Summaries that drop warnings/limitations.
3. Auto-delete originals — regret later.
4. Tag explosion.
5. Summarising instead of doing the task — procrastination cosplay.
