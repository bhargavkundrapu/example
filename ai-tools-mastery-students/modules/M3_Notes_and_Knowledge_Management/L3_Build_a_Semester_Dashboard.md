# Semester Dashboard

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M3-L3
- **Lesson Title:** Semester Dashboard
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 12 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can maintain a **dashboard** of deadlines, weights, and next actions — updated weekly.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M3-L3-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

NotebookLM-type grounded notebook + syllabus

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Treat knowledge like a tiny product: labels, dashboards, actions that survive exam week.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M3-L3-draft`.

---

## SEC-08_BAD_WORKFLOW

You paste exam paper screenshots into AI during closed prep. Integrity breach.

---

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> Model prints solution pattern you cannot reproduce.

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Export deadlines from syllabus into a table (manual trusted).
2. Add column: next physical action (30 min or less).
3. AI formats + suggests sort — not invent dates.
4. Pin “top 3 this week.”
5. Friday: mark done + roll incomplete with reason.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Here is my deadline table (paste). Make it readable + suggest a sensible sort. Flag missing fields [TBD].
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Given this week’s top 3 (paste), break each into 3 sub-steps ≤25m — I will edit times.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Write a kind “I need extension” email draft ONLY if I paste facts: course, assignment, due, reason, how much time — no invented excuses.
```

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. AI inventing due dates — **Verify** from PDF.
2. Dashboard with no next action — anxiety only.
3. Hiding big weights until night before.
4. Confusing calendar colour for work done.
5. Team tasks missing owner names.
