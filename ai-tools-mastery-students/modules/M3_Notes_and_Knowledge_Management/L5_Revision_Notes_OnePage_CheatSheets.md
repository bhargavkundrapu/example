# One-Page Revision Sheets

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M3-L5
- **Lesson Title:** One-Page Revision Sheets
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 18 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can build **one-page cheat sheets** that trigger memory, not replace it — exam policy permitting.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M3-L5-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

Grammarly-type writing assistant

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Treat knowledge like a tiny product: labels, dashboards, actions that survive exam week.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M3-L5-draft`.

---

## SEC-08_BAD_WORKFLOW

You demand slides Friday night without storyline first.

---

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> Deck is 62 bullets of sedation.

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. List 8–12 **high-yield** items only.
2. Use prompt cues, micro diagrams, edge cases.
3. AI suggests layout — you hand-copy once for muscle memory.
4. Test: hide sheet, recall 70%?
5. If open-book allowed, print; else use as pre-exam only per rules.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Topic list (paste). Design a one-pager structure: columns, symbols, warn icons. No micro-font essay — big headings.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Find redundancy: if two bullets teach same thing, merge suggestion only.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Give 5 “sheet edge case” questions I should add if I have room.
```

---

## SEC-13_GOOD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> 12-slide arc with presenter notes conversational.

---

## SEC-14_PRACTICE_TASK

One-pager for one unit. Recall test 24h later.

---

## SEC-15_CHALLENGE_TASK

Recreate sheet **blank** in 10 minutes. Compare gaps.

---

## SEC-16_QUICK_CHECKLIST

- [ ] - I used only permitted material.
- [ ] - I rewrote core ideas myself.
- [ ] - I marked uncertainties [VERIFY].
- [ ] - I checked college AI policy reminders.
- [ ] - I saved screenshots / links safely.
- [ ] - I did not paste exam questions verbatim.
- [ ] - I disclosed help if syllabus asks.

---

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Illegal cheat sheet in closed exam — don’t.
2. 6pt font — unreadable under stress.
3. Copying AI sheet verbatim — no memory trace.
4. Everything important — equals nothing.
5. No edge cases — same exam traps repeat.

---

## SEC-18_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Sheets are **prompts**, not encyclopaedias.
- Copying by hand aids recall.
- Policy check first.
