# Notes → Slides

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M6-L1
- **Lesson Title:** Notes → Slides
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 15 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can map **notes → slide deck skeleton** with one idea per slide before design noise.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M6-L1-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

ChatGPT-type assistant + textbook PDF

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Slides are storytelling + proof. Diagrams serve ideas — not decorate fluff.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M6-L1-draft`.

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Pick 10–15 key ideas max.
2. One headline + one visual/point per slide list.
3. AI suggests slide order — you defend story.
4. Move to design tool only after outline frozen.
5. Rehearse timing 1 min/slide test.

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Trusting citations AI “remembers”. **Verify.**
2. Uploading ID cards “for OCR fun.” Never.
3. Skipping the rewrite step.
4. Mixing teammates’ unpublished work.
5. Confusing polish tools with originality.
