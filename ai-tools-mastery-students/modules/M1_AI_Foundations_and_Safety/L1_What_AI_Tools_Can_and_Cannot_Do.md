---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can classify any study task into **helpful AI uses** (structure, explain, quiz) vs **harmful shortcuts** (graded answers you did not think through).

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M1-L1-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

ChatGPT-type assistant + textbook PDF

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

This module anchors **honesty** and **verification** — the premium advantage is finishing faster **without** fear.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Pick one task (e.g. “understand one subtopic”).
2. List what you need: definition, steps, one example, one common exam trap.
3. Ask AI for a **scaffold** — not a polished essay.
4. Close the tab. Rewrite the scaffold in plain words you would say to a friend.
5. Check one “hard fact” against your textbook index or lecture PDF (**Verify**).
6. Save a one-line rule: “AI helped with structure only.”

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
I am a college student. College rules on AI: I will follow them.
Topic (one line): __________
Give me ONLY: (a) what AI can help with here (b) what it should NOT do here (c) 3 honest questions I should ask myself before using AI.
No long essay.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Same topic. Give a **study scaffold** with: 1-sentence intuition • 3 numbered steps • 1 mini example • 1 “exam trap” bullet.
Mark any fact you are not 100% sure about as [VERIFY].
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Here is MY rewrite in my own words (I will paste next message). Do NOT rewrite it. Tell me: (1) one missing idea (2) one place I should verify in a source (3) one good follow-up question for my professor/TA.
```

## SEC-13_GOOD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

## SEC-14_PRACTICE_TASK

Pick a subtopic from this week. Run Prompt A + B. Rewrite to **8–12 lines**. Circle one claim and find the **page/slide** where it appears. If you cannot find it, mark it **unverified**.

## SEC-15_CHALLENGE_TASK

Teach the same subtopic **out loud for 90 seconds** without notes. Record audio. Then run Prompt C on a transcript you type from memory (not paste from AI).

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Asking “explain everything in the chapter” in one shot — you get fog.
2. Treating confident tone as truth — **Verify** numbers and names.
3. Skipping the rewrite — you learn almost nothing.
4. Using AI for live closed-book mocks — defeats the purpose.
5. Forgetting syllabus rules until submission night.
