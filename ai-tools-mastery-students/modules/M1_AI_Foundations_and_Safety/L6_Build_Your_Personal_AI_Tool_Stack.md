# Build Your Personal Tool Stack

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M1-L6
- **Lesson Title:** Build Your Personal Tool Stack
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 12 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can draw a **personal stack diagram**: capture → organise → draft → cite → revise — with **one** tool per step or intentional doubles.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M1-L6-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

Zotero-type citation manager + library database

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

This module anchors **honesty** and **verification** — the premium advantage is finishing faster **without** fear.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M1-L6-draft`.

---

## SEC-08_BAD_WORKFLOW

You let AI silently rename variables across files then push without testing. Brave.

---

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> Green locally because cache; red in CI.

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Map your real week: phone vs laptop availability.
2. Pick **one anchor** capture surface (notebook app or paper + photo).
3. Assign: AI role = explain/outline only; citation = manager; flashcards = SRS app.
4. Draw boxes on paper — photo optional.
5. Write “if one tool down, my backup is ___.”
6. Revisit stack after week 4 — simplify if overloaded.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Here is my weekly pattern (I describe). Suggest a **minimal** tool stack (max 5 tools) with one line each: job it does. If I should choose between two similar tools, say “pick one” with tradeoffs — mark prices [VERIFY].
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Critique my stack diagram description (paste). Find one redundancy and one missing link (e.g. citations, backups).
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Give a one-page “tool failure plan”: offline study, no AI, no cloud — 5 steps.
```

## SEC-14_PRACTICE_TASK

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Ten tools, zero habits — chaos cosplay.
2. Paying for premium before free workflow works.
3. No backup when Wi‑Fi dies.
4. Mixing team accounts and personal — boundary blur.
5. Letting stack replace sleep.
