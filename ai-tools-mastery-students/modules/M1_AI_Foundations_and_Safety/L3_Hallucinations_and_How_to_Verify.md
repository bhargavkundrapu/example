This module anchors **honesty** and **verification** — the premium advantage is finishing faster **without** fear.

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Ask AI for an explanation with explicit [VERIFY] tags on uncertain facts.
2. Split facts into: **in my materials** / **needs external check** / **opinion**.
3. For each “needs check”: open one primary source (textbook, paper, official doc).
4. Update your notes with **source pointer** (page, §, fig).
5. If you cannot verify in 5 minutes, mark **TBD** and move on — do not fake certainty.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Explain [topic] to me. Rule: after any specific claim (date, number, law, paper title, theorem name), append [VERIFY] unless you are citing a standard textbook fact you are very confident is universal — then say [COMMON] and still suggest I check my course book.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
I will paste 5 bullet “facts” from an AI answer. For each, label: LIKELY_TRUE / NEEDS_VERIFY / LIKELY_FALSE / OPINION — and say what evidence I should look for. If unknown, say [CANNOT_JUDGE].
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Turn my messy verification log (I paste) into a 2-column table: Claim | Where I will verify (source type only, not invented links).
```

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Trusting model “citations” without opening them.
2. Verifying only easy facts — skipping scary equations.
3. Using random blogs as “proof.”
4. Deleting [VERIFY] tags to look smart.
5. Panic-editing at 2 a.m. without sources.
