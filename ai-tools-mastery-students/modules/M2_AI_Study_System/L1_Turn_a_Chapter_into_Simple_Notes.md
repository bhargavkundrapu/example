## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Turn noise into routines: notes, quizzes, pacing. AI helps **structure**. You supply **truth**.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Skim headings + stop at one **target section**.
2. Without AI, write 6 messy bullets (“I think this means…”).
3. Paste those bullets + allowed excerpt (≤500 words) to AI.
4. Ask for: **definition / steps / one example / pitfalls** — nothing else.
5. Merge AI structure with your bullets — delete anything you cannot explain.
6. Add **source tag**: book p.__ or slide __.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
I have 6 rough bullets (paste next) + an allowed excerpt. Build a clean note with sections: Idea in one line • Steps • Example • Pitfall • “I should memorise”.
Do not add facts not supported by excerpt — mark gaps [GAP].
```

### Prompt B
```txt
Take my clean note (paste). Give 5 “connection questions” linking this section to prior chapters — questions only.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Compress my note to a **120-word** revision version. If you remove a detail, add a [LOST_DETAIL] tag so I notice.
```

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Highlighting whole PDF into AI — noisy + policy risk.
2. Accepting new “facts” not in excerpt.
3. Skipping messy-first step — notes feel alien.
4. Notes with no page pointer — useless in viva.
5. Perfectionism stopping at zero notes.

- **Messy-human-first** beats pretty-AI-first.
- Notes anchor to **page/slide**.
- Compression exposes what you actually remember.
