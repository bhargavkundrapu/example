# Outline → Draft

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** M5-L1
- **Lesson Title:** Outline → Draft
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 15 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can move **outline → draft** with AI on structure only until your voice exists.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M5-L1-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

---

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

ChatGPT-type assistant + textbook PDF

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Great marks come from clarity + honesty + citations — polish last, substance first.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

---

## SEC-07_SETUP_IN_2_MINUTES

1. Open your permitted assistant in a browser you trust.
2. Pin two tabs: **this lesson** + **official tool help** (*Verify pricing & data rules*).
3. Start a scratch note titled `M5-L1-draft`.

---

## SEC-08_BAD_WORKFLOW

You batch-upload copyrighted slides plus your ID scans “for context.” Convenience now, panic later.

---

## SEC-09_BAD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> “According to Wikipedia…” without page snapshot or date retrieved. Rubric shrug incoming.

---

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

Usually: **skipped verification**, **skipped policy**, **skipped personal rewrite**. Any one collapses marks or trust fast.

---

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Rubric → H2 headings.
2. Each H2: 3 bullets of evidence you will cite.
3. Draft **one section** messy.
4. AI: reorder bullets for logic — not replace.
5. Expand section by section yourself.

---

## SEC-12_COPY_PASTE_PROMPTS

### Prompt A
```txt
Rubric verbs (paste) → propose outline skeleton with section purpose lines.
```

### Prompt B
```txt
My bullet evidence under §2 (paste) — critique logic gaps only.
```

### Prompt C
```txt
Give a reverse outline from my draft (paste): one line per paragraph intent — flag drift.
```

---

## SEC-13_GOOD_RESULT_EXAMPLE

> Three bullets cite *your* lecturer PDF page + textbook equation number you checked.

---

## SEC-14_PRACTICE_TASK

Skeleton + one full section draft in your voice.

---

## SEC-15_CHALLENGE_TASK

Draft section with AI **closed** — compare quality/time.

---

---

## SEC-17_COMMON_MISTAKES

1. Trusting citations AI “remembers”. **Verify.**
2. Uploading ID cards “for OCR fun.” Never.
3. Skipping the rewrite step.
4. Mixing teammates’ unpublished work.
5. Confusing polish tools with originality.

---

## SEC-18_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Rubric-first outlines save rewrites.
- Reverse outline kills drift.
