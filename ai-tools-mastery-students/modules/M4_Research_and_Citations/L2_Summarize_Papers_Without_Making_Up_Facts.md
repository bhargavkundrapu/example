## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

You can **summarise** only what the PDF actually says — gaps stay visible.

---

## SEC-02_WHAT_YOU_WILL_BUILD

One tangible artifact for your current semester (notes page, outline, deck skeleton, test list, or dashboard section) saved as `M4-L2-output.md` or screenshots in your workspace.

## SEC-03_TOOL_FOCUS

Claude/Gemini-type assistant + handwritten notes photo (if allowed)

---

## SEC-04_WHAT_THIS_TOOL_DOES

Citation discipline beats panic citations. Teach tools to amplify **sources you possess**.

Tools here **accelerate scaffolding and critique**. They **never** certify truth. Policies differ by campus — **Verify** syllabus language. Facts that matter for grades get a second source (textbook page, paper DOI, official doc).

---

## SEC-05_WHEN_TO_USE

- You feel slow because the task is **messy**, not because you are “bad.”
- You already have **raw material** (PDF, lecture photo, half draft).
- You will still **rewrite in your voice** after AI helps.

---

## SEC-06_WHEN_NOT_TO_USE

- The assignment forbids AI **or** requires a closed-book exam mimic.
- You are tempted to paste **questions you must solve alone**.
- Uploading materials would leak **privacy** or **copyright** — check first.

## SEC-10_WHY_IT_FAILED

## SEC-11_GOOD_WORKFLOW

1. Upload or paste **allowed** chunks by section.
2. Ask for summary with [NOT_IN_TEXT] markers.
3. Cross-check every strong claim against PDF page.
4. Write your 5-line “so what” without AI.
