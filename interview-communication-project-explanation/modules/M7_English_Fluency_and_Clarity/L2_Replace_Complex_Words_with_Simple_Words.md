# Replace Complex Words with Simple Words

> 🧠 **Remember:** Swap table beats thesaurus panic.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M7-L2`
- **Lesson Title:** Replace Complex Words with Simple Words
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 15–23 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Build a personal swap list for interview answers to avoid foggy vocabulary.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Under pressure, fancy words often slip wrong meaning.

Plain words keep you honest and accurate.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

You want to say **leverage** seven times.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Swap Table (FANCY → PLAIN → EXAMPLE)

**Definition:** Replace ornamental words with plain verbs + one concrete example if needed.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “We leveraged synergies to leverage outcomes leveraging leverage…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Meaning collapse.
- Signals buzzword training over thinking.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “We reused the existing login module instead of rebuilding it—saved time. Example: we imported token checks from library X—only if true.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Plain verb: reused / saved.
- Example grounds meaning.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Quick swaps:** utilize→use | facilitate→help | commence→start | demonstrate→show | prior to→before

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Create **20 personal swaps** from your own writing samples.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Some fields need jargon?"
   **A:** Yes—define once simply, then use term consistently.


1. **Q:** "Interviewer uses heavy jargon?"
   **A:** Mirror carefully only if you understand—else clarify.


1. **Q:** "GRE words bad?"
   **A:** Not bad—only if precise and comfortable.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Write 3 keywords on sticky note before call—allowed glance.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Red pen edit:** take last answer draft—circle words >3 syllables—replace where possible.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Ask student-friendly behavioral question. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

List 10 words to simplify in my answer with replacements. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Over-simplifying technical meaning inaccurately.
- Infantile tone (‘me did code’) extremes—stay professional grammar.
- Confusing informal slang with simplicity.
- Flat monotone thinking simplicity equals boring—energy still okay.
- Erasing nuanced distinctions when precision truly required.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Plain language supports truthful precision.
- Swap tables accelerate editing under time.
- Examples rescue abstract words.
