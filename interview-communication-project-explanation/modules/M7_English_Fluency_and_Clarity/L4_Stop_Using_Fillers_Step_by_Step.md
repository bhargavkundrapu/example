# Stop Using Fillers Step by Step

> ✅ **Quick Win:** Track fillers—not shame—with a weekly decay chart.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M7-L4`
- **Lesson Title:** Stop Using Fillers Step by Step
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 16–23 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Reduce um/like/actually clusters using staged practice—not brittle perfection demands.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Heavy filler lowers perceived preparedness—even if substance strong.

Total elimination unrealistic for many humans under stress—that's okay (**Verify generalized claim** intuitively humane heuristic). Trend matters.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

High filler density under STAR answer.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Filler Replacement Ladder (COUNT → SWAP PAUSE → BREATH SLOT)

**Definition:** Count baseline, replace fillers with purposeful micro-pauses keyed to punctuation.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Um like yeah so basically like…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Signal noise ratio poor.
- Hard to follow actions list.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Pausing here—three actions: first… second… third… Result… *(short pauses)*”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Labels pause as intentional—reduces awkwardness.
- Numbering supports structure.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Rule:** pause <1s at commas.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Layer gentle finger counting hidden off-camera for enumerations.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Zero fillers possible?"
   **A:** Don't aim perfection—aim trend down weekly.


1. **Q:** "Fillers cultural?"
   **A:** Some pause norms differ—**Verify**—still optimize intelligibility.


1. **Q:** "Stutter vs filler?"
   **A:** Different—seek professional guidance if medical concern—compassion first.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Inhale through nose between numbered points quietly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Same answer thrice:** baseline / pause swap / slower final—graph filler count.
