## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Celebrate tiny trend—not binary success failure.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Perfection scheduling causing avoidance.
- Only typing never speaking aloud.
- Comparing selves to scripted influencers falsely.
- Neglect mental health burnout—professional help avenue **Verify** resources.
- Recording without consent illegally of others nearby—ethical mic hygiene.
