# When You Dont Know How to Respond

> > ⚠️ **Common Mistake:** Confident gibberish destroys trust slower than admitting partial progress.

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Maintain professionalism when stuck—using honesty + constructive partial reasoning.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Unknowns multiply in real jobs. Signals of prudent honesty matter.

**Verify:** Panels differ—some want struggle; clarify adaptively politely.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Hard unfamiliar concept appears mid-round.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Don't-Know Trio (BOUNDARY → PARTIAL → PLAN)

**Definition:** State what’s unknown cleanly, articulate partial intuition safely, propose next investigative step.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Confident fabricated mechanism OR total shutdown “I quit.”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Integrity failure path OR avoidance failure path.
- Shows zero collaborative debugging instinct.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “I’m not confident on _____ yet—I want to VERIFY definition precisely after. Partial intuition: _____ reminds me vaguely of _____ but I shouldn’t pretend certainty. Constructive next steps I’d attempt: sketch small example, brute force baseline, escalate learning resource ethically—during interview with your guidance preference?”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Names ignorance precisely without drama.
- Offers safe analogous intuition cautiously flagged.
- Asks interviewer guidance ethically—shows collaboration—not sneaky answer fishing beyond fair scope.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Stuck sandwich:** Unknown label + guarded partial angle + constructive ask.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Discuss resource learning plan post-interview—honest—not fantasy hero overnight mastery.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Should I guess wildly?"
   **A:** Label guesses explicitly as guesses—risk trade-off.


1. **Q:** "What if interviewer pushes bluff?"
   **A:** Kind firm boundary repeats VERIFY.


1. **Q:** "Mental blackout panic?"
   **A:** Breathing + narrow problem restate aloud—ethical calm reset.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Keep shoulders down while admitting unknown—confidence paradoxically steady.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Pick unfamiliar topic ethically (textbook appendix) practice **controlled ignorance** narration 45s loops.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Use intentionally tough domain prompt only if user consents in practice; forbid cheating leakage. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups. Do NOT provide solutions to graded tasks or leaked interview questions.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Judge honesty signaling vs needless self-sabotage phrasing extremes. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Apologizing identity-level (“I'm stupid”).
- Deflecting to unrelated boasting.
- Asking interviewer to solve fully for you—tone matters.
- Using AI mid live interview—typically cheating (**Verify hiring rules strictly**—this course does not authorize misconduct).
- Promising unrealistic mastery timelines.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Integrity under uncertainty is persuasive long-term.
- Partial scaffolding beats silence melodrama.
- Collaborative phrasing restores forward motion.
