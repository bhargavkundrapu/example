# Think Aloud Method for Coding Rounds

> 🧠 **Remember:** Label silence—“thinking”—beats empty freeze.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M5-L1`
- **Lesson Title:** Think Aloud Method for Coding Rounds
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 17–25 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Maintain transparent reasoning during technical tasks without pretending instant genius.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Interviewers often score *communication of problem solving* separately from correctness.

Silence reads ambiguous if not labeled.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Live collaborative editor with timer pressure.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Think Aloud Scaffold (ECHO → PLAN → TRY → AUDIT)

**Definition:** Speak what you observe, outline approach, trial with micro-tests, revise while narrating ethically.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> long silence … sudden code burst … monotone “done” …

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- No shared mental model.
- Surprise solution drop feels suspicious even if innocent.
- Can't course-correct interviewer assumptions.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Restating constraints: _____ . Brute baseline idea: _____ . Concern: time complexity _____ . Alternate: _____ Pattern check on sample _____ — pause while I simulate indices.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Observation share builds trust.
- Alternatives show maturity.
- Labeled silence reduces misinterpretation.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**30s think loop:** constraints + baseline + alternate + test plan utterance.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Add complexity guess as ‘tentative’ if uncertain—VERIFY formal naming if asked.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Stop talking—just code?"
   **A:** Clarify their preference politely—some panels differ.


1. **Q:** "Are you cheating from memory?"
   **A:** Stay truthful about prior exposure (**Verify institute honor code**).


1. **Q:** "Language choice?"
   **A:** Justify with readable intent—avoid dogma fights.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Keep mouse/cursor motions narrated sparingly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Solve easy puzzle on paper while recording voice—only narration practice.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Simulate interviewer observing think-aloud for a practice problem—not a leaked bank item. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups. Do NOT provide solutions to graded tasks or leaked interview questions.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Judge clarity of reasoning vs verbosity. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Over-explaining trivia wasting time.
- Masking cheating as think-aloud—this course forbids dishonesty.
- Insulting problem as ‘too easy’—arrogance risk.
- Ignoring interviewer hints repeatedly.
- Reading code without summarizing intention.

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Transparency de-risks misunderstandings.
- Alternatives show trade-off IQ early.
- Labeled silence is professional.
