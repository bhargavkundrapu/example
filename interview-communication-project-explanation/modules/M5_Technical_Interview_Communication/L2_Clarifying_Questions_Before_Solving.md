## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Too many questions annoy?"
   **A:** Batch compactly—then pause.


1. **Q:** "All constraints known?"
   **A:** State assumptions if forced—invite correction.


1. **Q:** "What if interviewer refuses clarifiers?"
   **A:** Assume minimal explicit defaults + SAY assumptions.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Slight upbeat tone prevents sounding confrontational.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Clarifier spam paralysis.
- Repeating interviewer words without new info.
- Asking disguised tutoring help beyond fair scope (**Verify fairness norms**).
- Passive aggressive tone when corrected.
- Arguing assumptions after explicit correction.
