# Explain Your Contribution Not We

> > ✅ **Quick Win:** Use “we” for context, **I** for your owned moves—mix consciously.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M4-L5`
- **Lesson Title:** Explain Your Contribution Not We
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 16–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Explain team projects without disappearing into ‘we,’ and without stealing credit.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Panels need to hire *you*. If everything is foggy ‘team effort,’ interviewers infer you did little.

If you hog credit, teammates references can hurt you (**Verify workplace norms**—this course forbids unethical guidance).

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“What exactly did YOU do?”** right after collaborative story.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Contribution Map Pronouns (WE context → I moves → CREDIT footer)

**Definition:** Fair team credit upfront, crisp personal actions list, teammate appreciation closing.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Everything… I coded everything… even design though not true…” OR “We everything…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Either theft or disappearance.
- No inspectable lanes.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “We built X together—three people.
My owned lane: routing module + unit tests + API contract doc with examples.
Teammates: UI visuals + database modeling + demo script.
If useful, walk through one PR/issue id I led—privacy permitting.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Team context fair.
- Personal lane enumerated.
- Others credited specifically.
- Evidence offer ethical.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**We/I pattern:** Two sentences WE + three bullets ME + credit line.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Add collaboration challenge showing shared ownership clarity.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Prove ownership?"
   **A:** Discuss allowed artifacts: commits/screenshots—with permission boundaries.


1. **Q:** "Conflict on credit?"
   **A:** Neutral process fix story—truthful.


1. **Q:** "Solo preferable?"
   **A:** Balanced honesty about collaboration skills.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Highlighter on README:** highlight lines you authored vs team—practice speaking map.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

"What did YOU do?" repeated twice with doubt tone. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Separate evidence vs vagueness sentences. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Using ‘we’ to hide laziness historically.
- Throwing teammate under bus.
- Listing tasks not done.
- Claiming infra access you lack.
- Posting private repo proofs without clearance.

- Credit fairness signals manager potential.
- Evidence offers must respect privacy/IP.
