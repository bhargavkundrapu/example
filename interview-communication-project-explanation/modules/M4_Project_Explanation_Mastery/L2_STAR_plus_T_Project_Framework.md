## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Walk a project using STAR+T with correct ordering and honest tech depth.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Explaining projects is a communication exam. Ordering signals whether you think like an engineer.

Stack-first answers often hide unclear thinking.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“Walk me through your project architecture.”** but you haven't drawn anything.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** STAR+T (add Technology after outcome clarity)

**Definition:** STAR for project stories; +T explains tools only where they support decisions you made.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “JWT… Mongo schema… Redux… hmm… many routes…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Architecture without user flow.
- No decision rationale.
- Listener cannot map mentally.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> **S:** Users forgot items frequently; admins lost messages.

**T:** Deliver tracked status + searchable records before semester demo.

**A:** Chose relational storage for predictable queries (truth); built CRUD endpoints; enforced role checks—claim only what YOU implemented.

**R:** Admin workflow shortened qualitatively; metrics VERIFY.

**+T:** Stack recap last: _____ languages/frameworks—I can explain auth trade-offs calmly.”

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Why not NoSQL?"
   **A:** Discuss trade-offs you actually debated—don't fake benchmarks.


1. **Q:** "Security?"
   **A:** Honest gaps + mitigation plan; VERIFY threat model basics if asked.


1. **Q:** "Scale?"
   **A:** Avoid fake millions of users unless true.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Pause before +T recap so it feels intentional.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Speak STAR without tech words for 45s → add +T recap 20s.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Project deep dive alternating between user story and tech justification. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Check STAR vs stack-first violation. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Saying microservices without need.
- Confusing tutorials with authored architecture.
- Co-authoring hallucinated contributions.
- Quoting complexities you can't derive.
- Tool fanboy tone instead of outcome tone.
