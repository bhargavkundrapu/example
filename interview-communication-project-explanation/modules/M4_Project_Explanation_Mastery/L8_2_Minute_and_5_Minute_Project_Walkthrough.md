# 2 Minute and 5 Minute Project Walkthrough

> ✅ **Quick Win:** Use the same truthful story spine—TIME shapes depth, not lies.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M4-L8`
- **Lesson Title:** 2 Minute and 5 Minute Project Walkthrough
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 18–25 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Deliver aligned 2-minute and 5-minute walkthrough versions without contradicting facts.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Different interviewers tolerate different densities. Boxes prevent rambling resets.

Also prevents inventing extras for ‘because longer.’

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Interviewer: **“We have limited time—two minutes.”** then later **“okay expand.”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Time-Box Spine (HEADLINE→MAP→ONE DEEP MODULE→BUG/RESULT→STACK)

**Definition:** 2-min: skim map + one jewel module. 5-min: fuller map + trade-offs + richer debug + honest metrics.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Same speed monologue squeezed—crashes coherence OR invents extras when expanded.

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- No modular expansion plan.
- Depth mismatch.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

**2-min truth pack:** headline + 3-module map + one interesting failure + recap stack summary.

**5-min add:** stakeholder/user nuance + second module cross boundary + richer trade-offs + cautious metrics language.

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Modular expansion scaffolding.
- Same facts—layered explanatory depth.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

Practice aloud with timers; record decibel clipping—avoid yelling when rushed.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Practice transition phrase: "If we add airtime, here's layer two:"

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Draw?"
   **A:** Offer simple boxes/arrows verbally or whiteboard politely.


1. **Q:** "Compare to competitor products?"
   **A:** Only if researched truthfully—not speculation dressed as certainty.


1. **Q:** "Biggest regret?"
   **A:** Architectural humility line—shows taste forming.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- First minute slightly slower anchors attention.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Twin timer reps:** Same project: 120s ×3, then 300s ×2 same day—not same wording required, same facts required.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Alternate 2-minute and 5-minute requests without warning twice. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Detect contradictions across lengths; forbid inventing expansions. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Speed-reading slides word-for-word.
- Introducing new teammate responsibilities when expanding.
- Inflating novelty for longer.
- No checkpoints for interviewer comprehension.
- Ignoring time warnings.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Timeboxing is truthful zooming—not lying zooming.
- Transition cues feel senior.
- Repetition with depth trains stability.
