## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Discuss results without hallucinated analytics; still sound competent.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Students often feel weak without KPI dashboards. Panels accept qualitative truth framed cleanly.

But fake KPIs wreck trust faster than admitting measurement gaps.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“What measurable impact?”** immediately after qualitative story.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Impact Compass (METRIC vs QUAL vs LEARN)

**Definition:** Deliver impact honestly: quantify if tracked; qualitative if measured loosely; learning if stalled.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Saved 93.7 hours per user per day worldwide…” *(nonsense)*

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Implausible magnitude.
- No measurement methodology.
- Sounds lying.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “We didn’t ship production analytics—I won’t invent KPIs.
Qualitative signals: admins reported fewer repeat messages qualitatively in supervised demo—VERIFY quoting.
If quant needed: we'd track median resolution time sample size N—planned but not finalized—truth.”

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Qualitative ethically framed.

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Impact triage:** quantify only if truthful; qualitative explicit; VERIFY call.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

**If truthful metrics**: method + timeframe + denominator + caveat.

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Why no analytics?"
   **A:** Deadline/student scope—not excuse for lying.


1. **Q:** "How verify later?"
   **A:** Logging plan responsibly—privacy respecting.


1. **Q:** "Business impact?"
   **A:** Discuss assumptions transparently—not fantasy boardroom math.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Lower pitch when disclaiming invented stats—steady not defensive.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Confusing hope with measurement.
- Cherry picking best day as average.
- Download counts inflated.
- Citing citations not read.
- Comparing unfairly without baseline.
