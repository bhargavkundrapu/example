# Explain Tech Stack Safely Only What You Know

> > ⚠️ **Common Mistake:** Calling yourself ‘full stack expert’ after one sprint—panels probe one level deeper and you stumble.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M4-L4`
- **Lesson Title:** Explain Tech Stack Safely Only What You Know
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 17–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Talk about stacks with explicit depth honesty: studied / used lightly / owns production behavior.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Misrepresentation is risk. Calibrated honesty often scores higher than bluff prestige.

Recruiters expect learning—not omniscience for early roles.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“Explain JWT to me like I'm new.”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Comfort Ring (LEVEL-0→3 Honesty)

**Definition:** For each tool: say what you personally did at each depth; stop before bluff radius.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “JWT is…” *copied textbook nonsense incoherent*

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Buzzword avalanche.
- Cannot answer follow-up simplest question.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “JWT is commonly a compact signed token the server verifies so clients can send stateless auth requests—conceptually that's my level understanding.
What I personally implemented: issuing token on login, storing refresh responsibly per our scope, validating on protected endpoints—anything deeper around key rotation VERIFY with my mentor.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Separates textbook concept vs personal execution.
- Names boundary of depth.
- Invites mentorship alignment.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Stack item answer:** Concept 1 sentence + Your usage 2 sentences + Depth boundary.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Discuss trade-offs you saw (stateless vs revocation complexity) honestly.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Revocation?"
   **A:** If you didn't solve it—say so + possible approaches.


1. **Q:** "Alternatives?"
   **A:** Sessions—compare at high level honestly.


1. **Q:** "Security mistakes made?"
   **A:** Truthful retrospective—shows growth.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Smile small when admitting unknown—prevents apology spiral.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Depth probe ladder on one stack item only. Stop if ethics broken. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Highlight bluff risk sentences. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Claiming infra you only clicked buttons on once.
- Confusing familiarity with competency.
- Memorizing definitions without runnable mental model.
- Attacking interviewer for probing.
- Name-dropping tools not in repo/readme truthfully yours.

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Comfort rings beat fake expertise.
- Depth boundaries earn respect.
- Concept + practice split clarifies honesty.
