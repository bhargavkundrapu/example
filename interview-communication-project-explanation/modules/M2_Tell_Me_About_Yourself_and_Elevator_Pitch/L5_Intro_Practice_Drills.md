# Intro Practice Drills

> 🧠 **Remember:** Drills beat inspiration. Tiny reps create stable voice under pressure.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M2-L5`
- **Lesson Title:** Intro Practice Drills
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 18–25 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Run a weekly intro rotation: 30s, 60s, JD-matched, and mild distraction conditions—without inventing facts.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Real interviews vary. If you only rehearse one script, small surprises break you.

Rotation builds flexibility while keeping truth constant.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Placement week: you have back-to-back panels. One panel is formal. One is casual. One interrupts early.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Rotation Drill Set (30 / 60 / JD / NOISY)

**Definition:** Practice the same truth in four formats so you don’t sound memorized—just prepared.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Bad example (fiction):_

> “Sorry I only practiced one script… can you repeat the question… I forgot…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Single-script dependency.
- Panicky repair instead of calm restate request (sometimes OK once).
- No plan for interruption handling.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Good micro-repair lines (truth stays same):_

> “Happy to keep this tight—30-second version: …”
> “I can expand the proof—pick API side or testing side.”
> “If you want me to pause for questions mid-way, I can.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Shows **format control**.
- Keeps **same evidence**.
- Invites collaboration—professional tone.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**30s + handoff** is your default weapon under stress.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

**60s** adds one coursework pillar + habit line.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Interrupted mid-intro—what now?"
   **A:** Pause, answer their point, resume with bridge: "Continuing my intro map…"


1. **Q:** "Panel looks bored?"
   **A:** Shorten, add one vivid detail (truthful), ask where to deepen.


1. **Q:** "Accent anxiety?"
   **A:** Slow down consonant endings—clarity beats fake accent attempts.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Train once with slight background noise to reduce fragility.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**7-day rotation (truth locked):**

| Day | Format |
|---|---|
| 1 | 30s clean |
| 2 | 60s |
| 3 | JD-matched |
| 4 | Record + count fillers |
| 5 | Friend interrupts once mid-way |
| 6 | Whisper draft then normal |
| 7 | Blind recall |

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Run 4-format rotation: interrupt once in format 4. Keep ethical bounds. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Score variability vs fakery risk: Did I sound like four honest angles or fabricated polish? Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Practicing only silently (typing) not aloud.
- Changing facts day-to-day (memory inconsistency).
- Over-practice until robotic monotone.
- No sleep before big day—voice breaks.
- Ignoring institute dress code / video background norms (VERIFY).

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Rotation preserves truth but upgrades adaptability.
- Micro-repair phrases save energy.
- Practice is rehearsal, not self-punishment.
