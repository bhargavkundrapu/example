# 60 Second Tell Me About Yourself

> 🧠 **Remember:** A 60-second intro is still not an autobiography—it's a guided tour with 3 stops.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M2-L2`
- **Lesson Title:** 60 Second Tell Me About Yourself
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 16–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Expand to ~60 seconds without drifting: three clear stops and a confident close.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Some panels want more than 30 seconds. If you don’t have a map, you add random detail and lose the thread.

Structure protects you more than ‘smooth English.’

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Interviewer says:

**“Take a minute—tell me about yourself with a bit more context.”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** 3-Stop Tour (NOW / PROOF / NEXT)

**Definition:** Stop 1: current identity. Stop 2: strongest proof. Stop 3: honest next step + invitation.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Bad example (fiction):_

> “So I was born in… then school… then many exams… then I joined college… many subjects… many friends… I did hackathons maybe… I know React… I know Java… I like AI… I want a good package… I will work hard…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Chronological drift without relevance filters.
- ‘Many’ is not evidence.
- Sensitive topic risk: compensation framing too early (varies by country—VERIFY norms).
- No clean third stop—ends like a wish list.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Good pattern (replace truthfully):_

> “I'm in final-year ECE with a software focus in projects. Strongest proof: our IoT energy monitor—my lane was firmware reading + basic cloud upload pipeline. I like problems where hardware meets simple reliable code. Next, I'm targeting embedded-lean roles and internships learning release discipline. I can go deeper on firmware testing or team workflow—your pick.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- **NOW:** stage + focus.
- **PROOF:** specific lane + integration surface.
- **NEXT:** honest direction without begging.
- **Close:** choice offer.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

_60 seconds scaffold:_

```
Now: _____ (degree + focus).
Proof: _____ — I owned _____ .
Why it matters (1 line): _____ .
Next: _____ .
Prefer firmware story or teamwork story?
```

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

_Stretch to ~90 seconds ONLY if interviewer explicitly encourages:_ add one failure-learning micro-line (truthful). Keep stack names bounded.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Why embedded?"
   **A:** Tie to real coursework/projects; avoid fantasies.


1. **Q:** "Biggest weakness?"
   **A:** Use M3 failure story framing later—here answer lightly with improvement plan.


1. **Q:** "Do you relocate?"
   **A:** Give honest constraints; VERIFY sponsorship/living costs separately from interview story.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Lift pitch slightly on the phrase “Strongest proof” so listeners notice the cue.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Stop markers:** Speak aloud inserting the words “Stop 1—Stop 2—Stop 3” privately on day 1. Remove markers day 3 while keeping pacing breaks.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Interview coach simulation: ask “Tell me about yourself—about one minute.” One follow-up. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Mark where my 3-stop tour blurred. Provide revised stop headings only. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

---

## SEC-15_SELF_EVALUATION_RUBRIC

| Dimension | 1–5 | What you're measuring |
|-----------|-----|-----------------------|
| Clarity | — | Listener can repeat your headline in one sentence |
| Structure | — | Moves are sequential, not scrambled |
| Confidence | — | Calm pacing, recovery after slips |

_Score 1–5 on each row. Pick your lowest score and rewrite one paragraph only._

---

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Listing twelve skills with no storyline.
- Oversharing personal struggles without a professional boundary you chose.
- Inflating leadership titles for group assignments.
- Running past 90 seconds without checking interviewer patience.
- Using salary words too early without prompt (VERIFY local norms).

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Three stops prevent life-story mode.
- Proof should be inspectable, not decorative.
- Next-step language can be honest and still strong.
