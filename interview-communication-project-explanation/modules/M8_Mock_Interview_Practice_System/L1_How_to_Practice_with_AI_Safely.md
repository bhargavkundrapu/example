# How to Practice with AI Safely

> > ⚠️ **Common Mistake:** Treating AI ‘facts’ about you as truth—it's not your memory.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M8-L1`
- **Lesson Title:** How to Practice with AI Safely
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 16–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Run AI mocks with guardrails so you strengthen communication—not invent a fake resume.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Practice partners are scarce sometimes. AI can help repetitions—but it can hallucinate praise or distort claims if you let it write your life story.

You keep ownership of truth.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

You paste resume; AI invents internship you never did if you ask “improve resume.”

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** SAFE AI Loop (FACT ANCHOR → ROLEPLAY → CRITIQUE → EDIT TRUTHFUL)

**Definition:** Anchor your real bullets first; AI critiques delivery; human verifies facts afterward.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Blindly copying AI achievements into resume/application.

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Use AI for: timed questions, probing follow-ups, delivery critique, readability edits **only** referencing provided truthful bullet anchors you typed explicitly **yourself**. After session: reconcile against evidence docs personally.

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Can AI simulate coding tests?"
   **A:** Not for cheating—practice only ethically transparent contexts.


1. **Q:** "Can AI simulate HR illegal questions?"
   **A:** Avoid fetishizing harm—consult trusted humans for sensitive realities.


1. **Q:** "Privacy of transcripts?"
   **A:** **Verify** tool data retention policies before pasting identifiers.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Announce aloud before session beginning: facts-only grounding reminder to yourself.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Create **Facts Sheet**: 25 bullet truths (projects, coursework, grades if shareable). AI must never expand beyond pasted facts.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

META: Teach safe mock loop only—don't roleplay unethical scenarios. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups. Forbidden: cheating, feeding hidden test answers, impersonation fraud, forging documents, spoofing identities.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Given my transcript anchored to pasted facts bullets, critique delivery only—not invent credentials. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Letting AI write cover letter lies.
- Pasting passwords/PII illegally carelessly.
- Using mocks to memorize deceptive scripts about skills absent.
- Replacing mentorship entirely—humans remain valuable.
- Ignoring academic misconduct rules.
