# What Behavioral Questions Are

> ✅ **Quick Win:** Behavioral questions test future behavior using past evidence.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M3-L1`
- **Lesson Title:** What Behavioral Questions Are
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 15–23 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Recognize behavioral intent behind common prompts so you answer with proof, not opinions.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Panels don’t trust adjectives (“I’m responsible”). They trust repeatable patterns shown through examples.

Your job is evidence, politely delivered.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Interviewer: **“Tell me about a time you missed a deadline.”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Intent Decoder (PROMPT → SIGNAL → STORY)

**Definition:** Read what habit the interviewer is buying: ownership, learning, teamwork, judgment—then pick a matching story.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Bad:_

> “Actually I never miss deadlines…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Sounds unrealistic for students with many constraints.
- Misses chance to show recovery and honesty.
- May trigger skepticism instantly.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

_Good pattern (truth-dependent):_

> “I’ll share coursework truthfully: we missed an internal review date because I underestimated integration testing. I escalated early with a one-page status, cut non-critical scope for the review, and finished the missing tests after. Outcome: review happened with a smaller feature slice; grade policy allowed late polish with penalty—VERIFY details if you need exact dates.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Names miss without drama.
- Shows early signal + scope negotiation.
- Keeps academic honesty + verification discipline.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

Missed deadline story in **STAR** with recovery + learning (no hero lies).

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Add teammates’ roles fairly—no blame theater; show your owned actions.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Whose fault?"
   **A:** Describe system failure: planning, communication, surprise—avoid personal attacks.


1. **Q:** "What would you do earlier next time?"
   **A:** Risk log + earlier test cut + weekly demo.


1. **Q:** "Any metric?"
   **A:** If a number (time saved, % improvement) is not verified, say VERIFY.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

List **10 prompts** neighbors got. Tag each prompt: Ownership / Conflict / Failure / Influence / Adaptation. Draft one headline story per tag.

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Ask 3 behavioral questions in sequence; increase pressure slightly on question 3. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

For each answer, classify intent fit and STAR gaps. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

|-----------|-----|-----------------------|
| Clarity | — | Listener can repeat your headline in one sentence |
| Structure | — | Moves are sequential, not scrambled |
| Confidence | — | Calm pacing, recovery after slips |

_Score 1–5 on each row. Pick your lowest score and rewrite one paragraph only._

---

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Answering with philosophy only.
- Using “we” for every action—hiding your contribution.
- Stories so long the listener forgets the question.
- Illegal/unethical stories presented as cool.
- Confidential employer details—VIOLATION risk.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Behavioral = evidence interview.
- Honest misses can build trust if recovery is real.
- Intent labels help you pick stories fast.
