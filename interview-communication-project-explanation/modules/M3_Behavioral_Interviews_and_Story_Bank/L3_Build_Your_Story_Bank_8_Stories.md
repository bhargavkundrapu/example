# Build Your Story Bank 8 Stories

> 🧠 **Remember:** A story bank is inventory, not memorized theater.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M3-L3`
- **Lesson Title:** Build Your Story Bank 8 Stories
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 18–25 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Create eight honest story cards you can trim and retag under pressure.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

One story cannot cover all prompts. A small bank prevents panic scrolling through your memory.

You’ll sound practiced because you practiced—not because you lie.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

Night before interviews: tired brain. Unexpected prompt: **“Tell me when you persuaded someone.”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** 8-Card Story Bank (TAG + HOOK + STAR-BULLETS)

**Definition:** Eight reusable stories tagged by skill signals; each card has a one-line hook + bullet STAR.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Uh… teamwork… everybody agreed… persuasion…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- No prebuilt persuasive moment.
- Generic teamwork blob.
- Can't prove.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Card tag: influence-without-authority. Hook: moved team from ‘ship fast’ to ‘add minimal logging first.’ Story: I showed two failure screenshots + time cost of blind debugging; proposed 1-day logging task; we adopted it; incidents became faster to localize—impact qualitative; VERIFY timeline if pressed.”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Tag helps retrieval.
- Hook is tweet-length.
- Story is truthful and bounded.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Make 8 hooks** (max 140 characters each truthfully).

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Expand 3 cards fully to STAR for deep interviews.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Can stories overlap?"
   **A:** Yes—retag responsibly; tweak emphasis per prompt.


1. **Q:** "What if I lack internship stories?"
   **A:** Use coursework, clubs, volunteer work—truth only.


1. **Q:** "Sensitive info?"
   **A:** Anonymize; avoid restricted data.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Create a **Notion/Google Doc** table: Tag | Hook | STAR bullets | Forbidden exaggerations list.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Rapid-fire 10 behavioral prompts; force me to name which story card fits. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Identify weak tags (too broad). Suggest sharper hooks without inventing events. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

---

## SEC-15_SELF_EVALUATION_RUBRIC

| Dimension | 1–5 | What you're measuring |
|-----------|-----|-----------------------|
| Clarity | — | Listener can repeat your headline in one sentence |
| Structure | — | Moves are sequential, not scrambled |
| Confidence | — | Calm pacing, recovery after slips |

_Score 1–5 on each row. Pick your lowest score and rewrite one paragraph only._

---

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Eight identical stories disguised differently.
- Hooks longer than paragraphs.
- Bank full of exaggerated wins.
- No failure story stored (many panels probe).
- No permission to shorten under time pressure.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Tags beat scrolling memory.
- Hooks save you on tired days.
- Honesty list prevents accidental lies under stress.
