# Quick Behavioral Answers PREP

> ✅ **Quick Win:** PREP is for fast answers—not for deep STAR stories.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M3-L6`
- **Lesson Title:** Quick Behavioral Answers PREP
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 15–23 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Deliver 25–45 second answers to quick HR-ish prompts using PREP without rambling.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Panels mix long behavioral questions with fast probes. PREP protects you when STAR would be oversized.

It also helps multilingual students stay linear.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“Why should we hire you?”** early screening

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point)

**Definition:** A crisp loop for short probes: headline, rationale, tiny example, recap point.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Because I'm the best…”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Arrogance without evidence.
- No match to posted needs.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

**P:** I fit three practical needs.

**R:** You want reliability basics + teamwork + willingness to learn—my stories map there without claiming expert mastery.

**E:** Example: I shipped coursework features with regression checks and communicated delays early.

**P:** So I'd add disciplined execution plus honest communication while I ramp—VERIFY wording with your onboarding expectations.

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Point sets scope.
- Reason ties to plausible needs—not fantasy.
- Example is truthful micro-proof.
- Point closes cleanly.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**PREP in 35s**—practice with timer.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

If interviewer asks STAR next, pivot: "I can give the longer story if you pick a theme.”

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "That's generic."
   **A:** Add JD keyword bridge—still honest.


1. **Q:** "What if not hired?"
   **A:** Stay professional—no begging; VERIFY follow-up etiquette.


1. **Q:** "Compare to others?"
   **A:** Avoid comparative trash talk—stay on strengths.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Thumb tap on thigh for PREP ticks (silent, subtle).

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

Prep **15 PREP prompts** flashcards—answer each in ≤40 seconds aloud.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

Rapid PREP drills: fire 12 prompts quickly. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Mark PREP drift (missing example, etc.). Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-15_SELF_EVALUATION_RUBRIC

| Dimension | 1–5 | What you're measuring |
|-----------|-----|-----------------------|
| Clarity | — | Listener can repeat your headline in one sentence |
| Structure | — | Moves are sequential, not scrambled |
| Confidence | — | Calm pacing, recovery after slips |

_Score 1–5 on each row. Pick your lowest score and rewrite one paragraph only._

---

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- PREP becomes another essay.
- Example unrelated to headline.
- Recycling one story awkwardly into every PREP.
- Using PREP when STAR is clearly expected—read the room.
- Guaranteeing outcomes you can’t promise.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- PREP is a rhythm, not magic.
- Short answers still need one proof bead.
- VERIFY keeps you honest under pressure.
