## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Emotional bleed OR unethical lies OR discriminatory reciprocation risk.

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Boundary-forward honest pattern: summarize fact neutrally, reason briefly if comfortable, emphasize role intent truthfully.
Example gap: caretaking responsibility—I'll keep private details minimal—but timeline closed now; readiness stable (**Verify**)—seeking _____ role continuity.”

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Maintain calm warmth—HR tone preference generally softer pacing (**Verify culturally**).

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

HR screening mixed sensitivity—respect boundaries ethically. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Jokes about unethical behavior—even ironically risky.
- Badmouthing previous boss toxically—even if truthful tone matters.
- Illegal discrimination mirrored back—consult professionals.
- Promising unethical flexibility on exam attendance etc.—never encouraged here.
- Performative diversity clichés hollow—prefer genuine respectful brevity.
