# How to Handle Followup Questions

> ✅ **Quick Win:** Follow-ups aren't attacks—they're sharpening tools.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M6-L1`
- **Lesson Title:** How to Handle Followup Questions
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 16–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Respond to unexpected drill-downs without panicking ramble or contradictory facts.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Stories compress first pass; drill-down probes truthfulness + depth.

Routing consciously saves face and time.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

After STAR story interviewer: **“But what metric proved success?”**

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Follow-up Router (SCOPE DOWN / SCOPE UP / SWAP STORY)

**Definition:** Choose to narrow details, widen context, or trade for a stronger truthful story politely.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Freeze OR invent KPI OR contradictory prior claim unintentionally.

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- No verification discipline.
- No structured branching.
- Panic collapse.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Good drill-down—we didn't formally measure _____ in class scope. Qualitative signal was _____ observable; if you want proxies, TA feedback noted _____ (VERIFY quoting). Alternate angle: secondary evidence _____ . Or I can instead expand technical implementation specifics—your preference?”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Acknowledges question value.
- Honest measurement boundary.
- Offers ethical alternatives—not dodge forever.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Router:** Admit gap → truthful substitute → directional choice.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Discuss why measurement absent without sounding defensive melodrama.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Sounds evasive?"
   **A:** Balance volume + specificity + evidence offers.


1. **Q:** "Contradicts earlier?"
   **A:** Repair explicitly “earlier overstated—I correct to…” truthful.


1. **Q:** "Rapid interruptions?"
   **A:** Micro-bridge phrases M1+M2 skills synergy.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

**Friend drill-down:** 30 random follow-ups—you answer with router only aloud.

---

## SEC-14_AI_MOCK_PRACTICE

### Prompt A (simulate interviewer ethically)

After each answer, escalate drill-down skepticism ethically. Rules: communication practice ONLY. Forbidden: cheating on tests/assignments, feeding hidden answers during live assessments. Do not invent my achievements—ask factual follow-ups.

### Prompt B (structure + feedback)

Flag contradictions or fake metric attempts. Rules: critique clarity, structure, honesty, pacing, confidence. Forbidden: rewriting my story with fabricated facts. Label anything unverifiable as VERIFY.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Over-apologizing for missing metrics—wastes oxygen.
- Hostile tone reacting to probing.
- Answering unasked new story—scope drift.
- Throwing teammate under hypothetical bus.
- Claiming confidentiality to hide dishonesty improperly.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Routing protects truth + momentum.
- Alternatives soften missing-data edges.
- Contradiction repair earns respect occasionally.
