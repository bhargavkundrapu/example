# How to Ask Good Questions to Interviewer

> > 🧠 **Remember:** Great questions reveal you think like someone who ramps—not someone who ghosts onboarding.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M6-L2`
- **Lesson Title:** How to Ask Good Questions to Interviewer
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Time:** 15–23 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Ask 4–7 high-quality end questions mixing role expectations, teamwork, evaluation, growth—without entitlement tone.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Panels note curiosity signal. Poor questions imply you didn’t prepare.

Good questions clarify mutual fit ethically.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

**“Any questions for us?”** with 90 seconds remaining.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** QUEST Framework (QUIT GOOGLE-able → EXAMPLES → SCALE → STACK → TEAM → TIME)

**Definition:** Ask non-lazy prompts showing you listened; avoid FAQs answerable purely from homepage generic.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “So… uh… perks… chai… cafeteria… vibes…” only OR “No.”

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Missed seriousness window OR sounded entitled OR lazy.
- No tie to conversational evidence earlier.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Two quick anchored questions:
Given you mentioned reliability pushes, what's the pragmatic definition of ‘done’ early for interns?
Second: mentoring format—reviews frequency + example growth path?
If time blur: asynchronous communication norms—preferred channels?”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Anchoring references earlier interviewer signals when truthful.
- Practical ramp lens.
- Culture asked respectfully—not entitled.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**3Q pack:** onboarding success + mentorship + norms.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

**7Q bank** rotated across companies—prep doc.

---

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "Is pay question okay?"
   **A:** **Verify:** salary/legal/visa truths vary massively by geography and employer—consult official sources/advisors—not this lesson. Usually better at later stage unless recruiter invites—defer politely.


1. **Q:** "Can I mention concerns?"
   **A:** Frame neutral curiosity—avoid insult.


1. **Q:** "Panel silent awkward?"
   **A:** Offer closing gratitude compactly.


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Slight leaning forward—not aggressive invasion baseline.

---

## SEC-13_PRACTICE_DRILL

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Humble-brags disguised questions.
- Interrogating panel aggressively.
- Overly niche legal advice fishing—risky (**Verify advisors**).
- Gossip about internal politics—avoid.
- Zero preparedness bank.

---

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Anchored questions outperform generic fluff.
- Onboarding framing shows professionalism.
- Time awareness matters at close.
