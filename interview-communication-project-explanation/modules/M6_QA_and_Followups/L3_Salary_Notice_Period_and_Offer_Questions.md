# Salary Notice Period and Offer Questions

> > ⚠️ **Common Mistake:** Quoting rumor salaries as facts—risky reputational blow.

---

## SEC-00_LESSON_ID

- **Lesson ID:** `M6-L3`
- **Lesson Title:** Salary Notice Period and Offer Questions
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Time:** 16–24 minutes

---

## SEC-01_SKILL_OUTCOME

Discuss notice period/relocation/comp expectations with neutrality + diligence prompts—without illegal/unethical coercion language.

---

## SEC-02_WHY_THIS_MATTERS

Poor tone here can disqualify—even if competence high.

**This lesson is NOT legal advice.** **Verify:** salary/legal/visa truths vary massively by geography and employer—consult official sources/advisors—not this lesson.

---

## SEC-03_REAL_INTERVIEW_SCENARIO

HR recruiter: **“What are your salary expectations?”** unexpectedly early.

---

## SEC-04_FRAMEWORK_YOU_WILL_USE

**Name:** Logistics Sandwich (RANGE NEUTRALITY → COMPONENTS → DOCUMENTATION)

**Definition:** Stay polite, factual about your constraints, VERIFY components, clarify documentation timelines—avoid myth stats.

---

## SEC-05_BAD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> Demands huge number OR coy zero OR insult pay OR cites fake friend's package as universal truth inaccurately.

---

## SEC-06_WHAT_WENT_WRONG

- Provokes unnecessary conflict OR signals cluelessness extremes.
- Spreads rumor stats as certainty.

---

## SEC-07_GOOD_ANSWER_TRANSCRIPT

> “Thanks for transparency—I'll share constraints neutrally. I'm early in process; I've researched broad ranges but **VERIFY** locality + role specifics with official recruiter guidance rather than rumor. Practical factors: _____ notice obligations / relocation constraints factual. Components question: Does range discussion include perks/bonus structure clarity later stage?”

---

## SEC-08_BREAKDOWN_LINE_BY_LINE

- Thanks + deferral ethically possible.
- Emphasizes verification vs rumor.
- Surfaces truthful constraints.
- Asks clarification components politely.

---

## SEC-09_SHORT_VERSION

**Salary micro-script:** neutrality + diligence + truthful constraints.

---

## SEC-10_LONG_VERSION

Discuss competing offers responsibly without lying—illegal fraud forbidden.

## SEC-11_FOLLOW_UP_QUESTIONS


1. **Q:** "They pressure exact number?"
   **A:** Cordial range openness if comfortable—never fabricate benchmarks.


1. **Q:** "Notice period lied?"
   **A:** Never instruct lying—ethical hazard.


1. **Q:** "Visa uncertainties?"
   **A:** Disclose ethically per forms—consult qualified advisor (**Verify**).


---

## SEC-12_CONFIDENCE_DELIVERY_TIPS

- **Voice:** speak clearly; emphasize names and truthful numbers slowly.
- **Pace:** slow down on technical words you can explain.
- **Pauses:** short silence beats extra “umm/like”.
- **Body / camera:** relaxed shoulders; stable camera; hands visible briefly.
- Breath before money words—steady tone.

## SEC-16_COMMON_MISTAKES

- Comparing coworkers' pay publicly unethically.
- Posting offer letters online—risk **Verify confidentiality** obligations.
- Committing verbally then ghosting employer—integrity harm.
- Threatening unethical leverage stories.
- Mixing placement training rumors as statistics.

## SEC-17_WHAT_YOU_LEARNED

- Neutrality reduces regret decisions.
- Verification language protects everyone.
- Constraints stated honestly early prevent mismatch pain sometimes.
